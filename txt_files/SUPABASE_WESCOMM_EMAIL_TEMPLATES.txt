WESCOMM Supabase Email OTP Templates
====================================

Why the email says "Confirm your email address"
-----------------------------------------------
If the email is new in Supabase Auth, Supabase sends the "Confirm signup" template first.
That is why the default email says:

"Confirm your email address"

This is normal. To make it look like WESCOMM OTP, edit both:

1. Confirm signup
2. Magic Link / OTP

Use the same WESCOMM-style OTP content below.

Where to edit
-------------
1. Open Supabase Dashboard.
2. Open your WESCOMM project.
3. Go to Authentication > Email Templates.
4. Edit "Confirm signup".
5. Edit "Magic Link / OTP".

Important:
- To show the email OTP, use {{ .Token }}.
- The code length depends on your Supabase Auth settings/project behavior. It may
  be 6 or 8 digits. Use the full code shown in the email.
- If you want OTP only, do not place {{ .ConfirmationURL }} in the email template.
- The first-time email may still be under "Confirm signup", but the wording can be WESCOMM OTP.

How to make the sender look real
--------------------------------
By default, Supabase sends from:

noreply@mail.app.supabase.io

You can change the email subject and template content, but for a real sender like:

WESCOMM <no-reply@wescomm.wesleyan.edu.ph>

you need Custom SMTP.

Supabase Custom SMTP setup
--------------------------
1. Open Supabase Dashboard.
2. Go to Authentication > SMTP Settings.
3. Enable Custom SMTP.
4. Use an email sending service such as Resend, SendGrid, Postmark, AWS SES, Brevo, or school-provided SMTP.
5. Set sender name:
   WESCOMM
6. Set sender email, example:
   no-reply@wescomm.wesleyan.edu.ph

For production, use a verified sending domain with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
